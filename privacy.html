Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026 · Updated October 19, 2026

Out of the Blue collects no data. Your contacts, and everything else, stay on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Out of the Blue (made by Hersh Applications) does not collect, upload, share, sell or transmit any personal information or usage data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code (SDKs). It does not connect to the internet.

Your contacts

With your permission, the app reads your contacts on your phone to pick someone for you to reach out to. It uses each person's name, nickname, photo, phone numbers and birthday to show their card, fill their name into messages and show upcoming birthdays. This information is read live from the Contacts app each time and is never uploaded or copied out of your phone.

If you share only some contacts (iOS "limited access"), the app sees only those. You can change this anytime in the iPhone Settings app or in the app's Settings › Contacts Access.

What the app saves on your phone

To work, the app keeps a small amount of its own information on your device:

  • An internal ID for people you've interacted with (not their name or number), plus whether you snoozed or removed them and when they last came up.
  • Your lists (for example "Family"), stored as those same internal IDs.
  • Your reach-out history: who, the date, whether you texted or called, and the message category.
  • Messages you write yourself, your favorite messages, and your settings.

This information never leaves your phone. It may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup, which Apple manages under its privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes it all. You can also delete history items in the app at any time.

Texts and calls

When you tap Text, the app opens Apple's Messages screen with the words filled in, and you choose whether to send. When you tap Call, it opens the Phone app. Out of the Blue never sends messages on its own and cannot read your messages, conversations or call history. It only learns whether you sent the text (so it can add it to your history), and after a call it asks you whether you got to talk.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your phone, such as a daily reminder or "It's Sarah's birthday today!" These are created on your device, not sent from a server. Birthday reminders include the person's name and may appear on your Lock Screen depending on your iPhone's notification settings.

Children

Out of the Blue is not directed to children under 13. Because it collects no personal information, there is nothing for us to review, export or delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We will never start collecting your information without first updating this policy.
