Out of the Blue

Reach out to someone you haven't talked to in a while

Tap Shuffle and a deck of your contacts slows down and lands on one person. Then send them a warm, ready-made text (encouragement, a Bible verse, "I'm praying for you," or your own words) or give them a call. The app fills in the words; you always tap Send.

Good to know

  • Everything stays on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no servers.
  • Faith messages (Bible Verses and Praying for You) can be turned off anytime in Settings.
  • Make lists like Family, Church or Work and shuffle just that group.
  • No streaks or scores. Just a gentle count of who you've reached out to this month.

Common questions

Why does the app need my contacts? To pick someone for you to reach out to and to show their name, photo, number and birthday. Your contacts are read on your phone only and are never uploaded.

I only shared some contacts. How do I add more? Go to Settings › Contacts Access › Choose More Contacts, or tap Share More Contacts in Lists › All Contacts.

Someone keeps coming up that I'd rather not see. On their card, tap Snooze (1 week, 1 month or 3 months) or Remove. You can bring anyone back from Settings › Hidden People.

Why doesn't the app send the text for me? On purpose. It opens Messages with the words filled in so you can read them and tap Send yourself.

Someone never comes up in a shuffle. Only contacts with a phone number can come up. People you reached out to recently are also skipped for a while (2 weeks by default, changeable in Settings).

I'm not getting reminders. Check Settings › Reminders in the app, then make sure notifications are allowed for Out of the Blue in the iPhone Settings app. Open the app now and then so it can plan the next two weeks of reminders.

Is my history backed up? Your lists and history are saved on your phone and included in your normal iPhone backup. Deleting the app deletes them.
